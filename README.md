# Locusable OSS
We ship open-source apps people actually ask for—sourced gaps, ranked by votes, delivered as installable builds and source.

## Mission
Close real product gaps in the open. Every app here starts as a documented demand, moves through a public board, and leaves with a downloadable release—not a pitch deck.

## Goals
- Keep the pipeline honest: evidence in, votes up, progress visible.
- Prefer small, sharp tools over feature catalogs.
- Publish under `locusable-oss` so source and binaries stay findable together.

## Milestones
- NextTo is live: the public demand board that feeds this org.
- LocusCal shipped (`v2026.9.16`)—calendar gaps closed end to end.
- LocusUpdate is in the pipeline under this org.

Browse demands and downloads: https://nextto.locusable.com  
Studio: https://locusable.com

# Locusable OSS
我们做的是「有人喊缺、按赞排期、能下载拿走」的开源应用——缺口有出处，进度公开，交付是安装包和源码。

## 使命
把真实产品缺口在开源里做完。这里的每个应用都从可核对的需求出发，经公开看板推进，最终带着可下载的版本离开——不是停留在概念页。

## 目标
- 流水线保持诚实：证据进、票数上、进度可见。
- 工具短而准，不做功能堆砌。
- 统一放在 `locusable-oss`，源码与安装包同处可查。

## 里程碑
- NextTo 已上线：喂养本组织的公开需求看板。
- LocusCal 已发版（`v2026.9.16`）——日历类缺口走通全流程。
- LocusUpdate 已进本组织流水线。
